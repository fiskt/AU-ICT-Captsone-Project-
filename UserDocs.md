# Getting Started

## Account Creation

1. Open the management base in your browser
2. Go to the registration page
3. Select your role
   * Coach accounts require a `@tennis.com.au` email address
4. Enter your first name, last name, email address, and password
5. Click **Register** — a verification email will be sent to the address you provided
6. Open the email and click the verification link. You will be directed to your dashboard

## Logging In

1. Go to the login page
2. Select your role
3. Enter your email and password
4. Click **Sign In**

## Forgot Password

1. Click **Forgot Password**
2. Enter the email address you want the reset link sent to
   * An email with a password reset link will be sent to that address
   * The reset link expires within 1 hour
3. Click **Reset Password** in the email — you will be directed to a password reset page
4. Enter your new password
5. Click **Update Password** to confirm the change

## Logging Out

1. Click on your profile at the bottom of the sidebar
   * On mobile, open the sidebar using the menu button on the left of the top bar
2. Click **Log Out**

## Manage Your Account

1. Go to **Account Settings** in the sidebar
2. From that page you can change your profile settings or delete your account

# Coach Interface

## Dashboard

**Athlete Filter**

* Use the dropdown menu in the top right corner to switch between viewing stats for all athletes or a specific player

**Planned vs Actual RPE Chart**

* Compares planned RPE against actual RPE
* Each node represents a session in the selected week
* Use the previous week and next week buttons to change the selected week
* Click **This Week** to jump back to the current week

**Training Intensity Zones**

* View player feedback for sessions in each intensity zone

**Weekly Activity Panel**

* Shows a list of sessions for the current week
* Click a session card to open it in the calendar page

## Calendar

### Creating Sessions

1. Go to the calendar page
   * Desktop: use the week calendar view
   * Mobile: use the day calendar view
2. Open the session creator
   * Desktop: click the orange **+** button on the right side of the page
   * Mobile: click the **Add Session** button
3. Enter a session name, RPE, and optional notes
4. Set the session times
   * Desktop: drag and drop the session icon into the calendar to set the start time (default duration is 1 hour) — this is done in Step 7
   * Mobile: set the start time and duration using the dropdown menus under **Start Time** and **Duration**
5. Under  **People** , select the coaches and players to include in the session
   * At least one coach and one player must be selected
6. Optionally, add drills from the drill library to the session under **Drills**
   * Click the **x** button on a drill card to remove it
7. Create the session
   * Desktop: drag the session into the calendar for the time slot you want
   * Mobile: click **Add to Calendar**

### Editing Session Details

1. Click an existing session in the calendar to open the session editor
2. Update any fields in the editor
3. Click **Save Changes** to apply updates, or close the editor to discard changes

### Editing Session Times

* **Desktop:** drag the session to a different time slot to change the start and end times; drag the bottom of the session event to change the duration
* **Mobile:** click the session to open the editor, change the start time and duration, then click **Save Changes**

### Deleting Sessions

1. Click an existing session to open the session editor
2. Click **Delete** and confirm the deletion in the prompt
   * Deleting a session removes it for all assigned participants

### Sending Email Notifications

1. Click the **Notify** button and click **Send** in the prompt to send email notifications for all sessions that haven't been notified yet
   * A mail icon will appear on session events that haven't been notified

## Player Profile

### Finding a Player

1. Use the search bar to search for specific players
2. Use the **A-Z / Z-A** dropdown to sort the list
3. Click **Details** on a player card to open their details page

### Strengths & Weaknesses

* Each player can have up to 5 strengths and 5 weaknesses
* Click the **+** button to add a strength or weakness
* Click the **x** button on a strength or weakness to remove it

### Logging Injuries

1. Click **Report Injury** to open the injury form
2. Fill in the injury type, body part, optional description, severity, and date occurred
3. Click **Submit** to log the injury

### Managing Existing Injuries

1. Click **Manage** on an existing injury card
2. Update the injury status, date recovered, notes, and training restrictions
3. Click **Save Changes** to apply updates, or cancel to revert

### Deleting a Player

1. Click **Details** on the player card
2. Click **Delete Player** and confirm the prompt

## Drill Library

### Creating Drills

1. Click **Add Drill** to open the drill creation form
2. Enter a drill name, select the difficulty level, and write a description
3. Optionally, select or create tags and add notes
   * Add a new tag by typing a name and pressing Enter in the tag input box
4. Click **Save Drill**

### Editing Drills

1. Open the drill editor
   * Hover over the drill card and click the pen icon, or
   * Click the drill card and click **Edit Drill**
2. Make your changes and click **Save Changes** to apply, or close the editor to cancel

### Deleting Drills

* Open the drill editor and click  **Delete** , or
* Hover over the drill card and click the bin button

### Managing Drill Tags

1. Click **Manage Tags**
2. View which tags are in use and delete any as needed
3. Click **Done** to close the overlay

## Other Users

### Selecting a User

1. Use the search bar to find specific users; use the coach/player filters to narrow results
   * Click the **x** button to reset the search bar and filters
2. Click a user in the list to load their session data into the calendar
3. On mobile, click **Show Less** to collapse the user list and **Show More** to expand it

### Session Details

1. Make sure a user is selected
2. Click a session event in the calendar to open the session details overlay
   * Changes cannot be made to sessions from this page

## Performance Testing

### Entering Test Results

1. Click **Add Test Result**
2. Select the player, date, gender, and PHV state
3. Fill in the results for the tests
   * At least 3 test results are required for the system to generate a chart on the details page

### Viewing Test Details

1. Use the search bar or dropdown menu to filter results for a specific player
2. Click a results card to open the details page for that test

### Deleting Test Results

* Click a test card to open the details page and click  **Delete** , or
* Hover a test card and click the bin button

# Player Interface

## Dashboard

**Upcoming Session**

* Shows a card for the next session you're involved in
* Click the card to open it in the calendar

**Weekly Activity**

* Shows a list of sessions for the current week
* Click a card to open it in the calendar

**Coach Updates**

* Shows the latest changes made to sessions you're in

**Quick Feedback**

* Feedback form for sessions that need to be rated
* Select an intensity level, enter the actual duration, and add optional comments
* Click **Submit Feedback** to submit

## Session Feedback

### Rating a Session

1. Click a session card in the **Sessions to be Rated** panel
   * If there are no sessions listed, you have already rated all your sessions or had no sessions to rate
2. Fill in the feedback form with intensity, actual duration, and optional comments
3. Click **Save Feedback**

### Editing Feedback

1. Click a session card in the **Rated Sessions** panel
   * If there are no sessions listed, you haven't rated any sessions yet
2. Make your changes
3. Click **Save Feedback** to apply the changes

## Calendar

* Shows sessions you are involved in as calendar events
* Click an event to view the session details
* Sessions cannot be modified from this page

## Injury Log

1. Go to **Account Settings** in the sidebar
2. Open the **Injuries** tab
3. Click **Report Injury** and fill in the form to log an injury
