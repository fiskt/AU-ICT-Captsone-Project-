### Registration

Registering as a coach requires the user to have access to an email with the domain "tennis.com.au"

### Coach Dashboard

**Player dropdown**

* Use the dropdown to select a player and view their sessions and RPE

**Session RPE graph**

* Displays the planned and actual RPE of the selected week for the selected player

**Weekly activity**

* Displays a list of sessions that the selected player is participating in
* Click on the cards to enter the session editor in the coach calendar

**Training intensity**

* Displays the nunmber of sessions the selected player has for each level of RPE
* Click on the cards to open the feedback page

### Coach Calendar

**Calendar functions**

* Select months/weeks/days using th arrows on the left
* Click the today button to jump to the current month/week/day
* Choose the calendar view using the buttons on the right

**Session creator**

* Enter the name, RPE, notes (optional) and select participants and drills (optional)
* Sessions require name, valid RPE (must be > 0) and at least 1 coach and 1 player for participants
* Drag the valid session into the calendar to schedule it. Session creation is only available in the week view for desktop

**Session editor**

* Click on a session in the calendar to open the editor for that session
* Session name, valid RPE and participant requirements are the same as the session creator
* Click save changes to save any changes you made to the session or close the editor to cancel the changes
* Click delete to remove the session

**Session times and duration in desktop view**

* Hover over the bottom of the session to resize the box. This changes the session   duration
* Click and drag a session to change the day its in and its start time

**Mobile session creator and editor**

* Drag and drop is disabled for mobile devices and the session creator is only available in the day view
* Set the session time in the creator or editor through dropdowns

**Session email notifications**

* Email calendar invitations can be sent to all participants of a session by clicking the notify button
* Session nofifications are sent manually and can only be sent once
* A mail icon will appear for sessions that have not had a notification sent yet

### Player Profile

**Player selector**

* Shows a list of all players
* Click on details to view the players details

**Player details**

* Click delete to remove the player from the system
* Click + on the strength or weakness sections to add a strength or weakness, up to 5 each
* Click the x on the strength or weakness to remove them

### Other Users

**User seletor**

* By default, no user is selected
* Filtering by coaches shows only coach users, filtering by players only shows player users. Resetting the filters clears the search bar and coach/player filters
* Select a user and the sessions they are participating in will show on the calendar

**Session details**

* Click on a session in the calendar to view the session details
* Users cannot change session details in this page

### Preview

* Clicking preview will open a selction menu where users can select a player dashboard to preview
* Click exit preview to return to the coach dashboard

### Accessing Your Account

* Open the options menu by clicking on your name at the bottom of the sidebar. You can log out of your account or access your settings
* In your account settings, you can change your name, date of birth and gender or delete your account
* To access your account on mobile, open the sidebar with the button at the top
